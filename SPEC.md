## THE PROBLEM

People have a hard time thinking of what to create, let alone creating it. Every existing tool assumes you already know what you want. Jasper needs a prompt. Canva needs a template choice. Midjourney needs a description. The blank page problem is unsolved.

Mwhile, 63% of organizations expect agentic AI to free up creative time (Adobe 2026). 42% plan to design distinctive AI7 agent personalities. The market wants agents that CREATE — not just assist.

## THE INSIGHT

What if the agents ideate FOR you? What if they COMPETE to pitch the best idea, and you watch the show? Then the winning idea gets built by specialist agents, protected with invisible ownership stamps, and deployed to the web in one click?
NObody has this. Current tools are either:
- Single-purpose (text OR images OR video ‐ never all)
- Generic LLM wrappers (no personality, no competition, no provenance)
- Enterprise workflow tools (not creative-first)
- Missing protection, measurement, and deployment

FRAYMUS has ALL the pieces: 5-agent fleet, Arena competition system, Free Studio (music/images/video), Castifyd (podcasts), Fraynix engine (DNA/WASM/steganography), VPS deployment, GitHub integration, and honest brain measurement (Babel trust tables, Brier scores).
